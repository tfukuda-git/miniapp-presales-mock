Core Loop

👆 Swipe to Discover Jobs

Users swipe through job cards one at a time. Right = Like, Left = Nope. Simple, fast, addictive.

😊 "It's like Tinder but for jobs — fun to use!"
ME Integration

Right swipe → add_favorite event
Left swipe → swipe_nope event
9 params per event (jobID, category, salary, location, tags…)

② Dopamine Design

💚 Instant Like Reaction

After swiping right, a toast appears: "Liked! Looks like you enjoy this type of work ✨"

Small dopamine hit → unconscious continuation → higher session length.

😍 "The feedback feels good — I want to keep swiping"
Purpose

Retention driver. Micro-feedback loops increase daily swipe count by 20-30% (industry benchmark).

③ AI Learning Core

🧠 Why Did You Like This?

Occasionally after a LIKE, we ask "What caught your eye?" with 4 quick options. One tap, done.

👍 "Just picking one option — easy enough"
ME Integration

Selection → like_reason event
Params: jobID, reason (salary / location / shift / no_exp)
→ Feeds into ME recommendation engine to improve match quality

Negative Signal

🤔 Nope Feedback

Every 5th left swipe, ask "What didn't fit?" to capture negative preferences.

👍 "They're actually listening to what I don't want"
ME Integration

Selection → feedback_submit event
→ ME custom attributes updated with negative preferences
→ Suppresses similar jobs in future recommendations

④ Daily Retention

⭐ Today's Recommended 5

AI-curated daily picks based on swipe history. Match scores show relevance. Creates a daily habit loop.

👍 "Just 5 to check today — I can do that"
ME Integration

Powered by ME recommendation engine.
browse_product events from recommendations tracked separately.
Match scores derived from user's event history + catalog attributes.

⑧ Re-engage

🔔 Re-engagement Push

When a user goes dormant, send "New jobs matching your taste!" via LINE push. Resurface previously liked patterns.

👍 "Oh right, I should check the new jobs"
ME Integration

ME Push delivery based on dormancy triggers.
Deep link → opens mini app directly to "Today's Picks" screen.
"Similar to a job you liked" tags powered by ME matching.

⑤ Addiction Design

⏰ Daily Swipe Limit (20)

After 20 swipes: "That's a wrap for today!" Shows liked count and invites to review history. Creates artificial scarcity.

👍 "I'll come back tomorrow to see more"
Purpose

Dating app playbook: scarcity → anticipation → daily return.
Combined with next-day push notification for maximum re-engagement.

⑦ Safety Net

📋 History & Review

Users can revisit both liked and skipped jobs. Reduces "what if I missed something" anxiety. Skipped jobs can be re-liked.

👍 "I can always go back — no pressure"
Purpose

Prevents opportunity loss. Users who review history have 2x higher apply rates (industry data). Skipped list enables "second chance" recommendations.
